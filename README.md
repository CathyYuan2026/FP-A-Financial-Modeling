# FP&A Financial Modeling

Practical FP&A and corporate finance examples demonstrating financial modeling, forecasting, variance analysis, scenario planning, cash flow analysis, and management reporting.

## About This Portfolio

This repository contains illustrative financial models and FP&A work designed to demonstrate practical approaches to financial planning and analysis.

All examples use fictional or anonymized data and are created for demonstration purposes only.

## Areas Covered

- Rolling Forecasting
- Budgeting & Forecasting
- Driver-Based Financial Modeling
- Budget vs. Actual Variance Analysis
- Cash Flow Forecasting
- Scenario & Sensitivity Analysis
- Management Reporting
- KPI Analysis
- Financial Decision Support

## Featured Project

### Rolling Forecast & Scenario Analysis

An illustrative FP&A model demonstrating how operating assumptions can be translated into financial forecasts and management insights.

The model will include:

- Actual vs. forecast analysis
- Driver-based revenue and expense forecasting
- Rolling financial forecast
- Cash flow projection
- Base, upside, and downside scenarios
- Variance analysis
- Management dashboard

## Professional Approach

Financial models should support decision-making, not simply produce numbers. My approach combines financial analysis, business understanding, structured modeling, and professional judgment.

AI can improve the efficiency of financial analysis and modeling, while assumptions, calculations, outputs, and conclusions still require professional review.
